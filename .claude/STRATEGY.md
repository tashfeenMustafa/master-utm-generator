# Master UTM Generator — Strategic Vision & Positioning

## The Core Insight

You've built a tool for marketing ops people. You should monetize them. But you should market to small business owners.

**Why this works:** The small business owner reads your educational content, shares it, and drives traffic. The marketing ops person sees it, needs the tool, and buys it.

## Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

### ICP B: Marketing Ops / Agency (Primary Revenue)
- **Who:** Marketing ops managers managing 3-8 clients, or agencies with shared processes
- **Problem:** Team generates inconsistent UTM naming. Tired of spreadsheets. No single source of truth for naming conventions
- **What they have:** Airtable, Google Sheets, maybe Monday.com. Team of 2-5
- **Price sensitivity:** $15-39/month is affordable
- **Buying signal:** "How do we enforce consistent UTM naming across clients?"
- **Conversion time:** 2-4 weeks from awareness to trial to purchase
- **Lifetime value:** High (12+ months if product sticks)
- **Volume needed:** 40-55 paying workspaces at $19/month = $45K ARR

### ICP A: Non-Technical Small Business Owner (Content Audience)
- **Who:** Freelancers, local service providers, e-com founders running Meta/Google ads themselves
- **Problem:** Campaign numbers don't match. No idea what UTM parameters are. Guesses on best practices
- **What they have:** Google Analytics (might not have looked at it), a Google Sheets budget, maybe Canva
- **Price sensitivity:** $9-12/month possible but tight. More likely: Free forever with basic features
- **Buying signal:** "Why don't my UTMs match my ad platform?"
- **Conversion time:** 6-12 weeks of consistent content before they even know you exist
- **Lifetime value:** Medium (4-6 months before churn if free, longer if education keeps them engaged)
- **Volume needed:** 350+ paying users at $12/month = $50K ARR

---

## Why ICP B First

1. **Monetizes in 6-8 weeks** vs. 3-5 months for ICP A
2. **Higher LTV** — ops people stay, small business owners churn after the initial campaign
3. **Viral acquisition loop** — ICP A reads your content → shares → ICP B sees it → buys

## Dual-Motion GTM Strategy

### For ICP B (Product-Led, Targeted Outreach)
1. **Launch** → Direct outreach to 50 marketing ops people (LinkedIn, Twitter, email)
2. **Free trial** → 14-day access to full Pro features (unlimited links, sync, CSV)
3. **Pricing** → Pro $15/mo (single user), Agency $39/mo (team + shareable library)
4. **Activation** → Help them set up Airtable/Sheets sync in first 3 days
5. **Retention** → Monthly email: "Here's how [agency name] uses UTM Generator"

### For ICP A (Content-Led, Organic)
1. **Educational content** → Blog + Twitter thread series: "Why Your UTMs Are Broken", "Copy This UTM Structure"
2. **Lead magnet** → Free UTM health checker (paste a URL, get feedback)
3. **Organic growth** → SEO-friendly guides on UTM best practices + screenshots of the tool
4. **Conversion** → Small % of ICP A users buy Pro for features like QR codes, bulk generation
5. **Compounding** → Each piece of content drives 20-30 users/month; 5% stick = 1 paying user
